# Konesans question bank — writing brief

Konesans is a quiz app for Haitian kids aged 6–14. Kreyòl ayisyen is the primary language, French the second. You are writing authored quiz questions as data. A Haitian reviewer will proofread every line in an admin panel, so **status is always "review"**.

## Output format
One JSON file per category at `/home/claude/konesans/content/bank/<category>.json` — a JSON **array** of objects, one object per line (so diffs are readable). Keys:

```json
{"id":"ist-001","d":1,"ages":["6-8","9-11"],"ht":"Ki kapital Ayiti?","fr":"Quelle est la capitale d'Haïti ?","opts":["Okap","Pòtoprens","Jakmèl","Gonayiv"],"optsFr":["Cap-Haïtien","Port-au-Prince","Jacmel","Gonaïves"],"a":1,"ex":"Pòtoprens se kapital Ayiti, nan depatman Lwès.","exFr":"Port-au-Prince est la capitale d'Haïti, dans le département de l'Ouest.","topic":"depatman"}
```

- `id`: category prefix + 3 digits, unique, sequential (prefixes: istwa→`ist`, jewografi→`jeo`, kilti→`kil`, syans→`sya`, matematik→`mat`, jeneral→`jen`, espo→`esp`, teknoloji→`tek`).
- `d`: difficulty 1 (6–8), 2 (9–11), 3 (12–14). `ages` lists every band the question may be served to (a d=1 question is often fine for all three bands; a d=3 question is usually `["12-14"]`).
- `ht` / `fr`: the prompt. Kreyòl ends with `?`; French uses a space before `?`.
- `opts` / `optsFr`: 3 or 4 options, same order in both languages. Exactly one correct. Distractors plausible, same type/length as the answer. Never "tout repons yo" / "okenn".
- `a`: 0-based index of the correct option. Vary it (don't always put the answer at index 1).
- `ex` / `exFr`: one sentence shown after answering (why it's right, or a fun fact). Required.
- `topic`: a short tag (lowercase, no spaces) for grouping in the admin panel.

Validate before finishing: `node -e "const a=require('/home/claude/konesans/content/bank/X.json');console.log(a.length)"` and `node /home/claude/konesans/tools/check-bank.mjs` (reports duplicates, bad indexes, orthography warnings).

## Kreyòl orthography (official, Akademi Kreyòl Ayisyen)
- Letters: no `c` (except `ch`), no `q`, no `x`; use `k`, `s`, `w`, `y`, `z`. `ou`, `ui`, `an`, `en`, `on`, `è`, `ò`, `à`, `ui`.
- Short pronoun forms are written with a **space, no apostrophe**: `m ap`, `l ap`, `yo t ap`, `pa t`, `nou pral`, `ki sa l ye`.
- Nasal marks: `anpil`, `lonbrit`; the n-with-dash for non-nasal `àn`/`èn`: `pàn`, `Lavàn`, `fèn`.
- Numbers with words: `1ye janvye 1804`, `18 novanm 1803`. Months: janvye, fevriye, mas, avril, me, jen, jiyè, out, septanm, oktòb, novanm, desanm.
- Capitalize proper nouns only. Place names in Kreyòl: Pòtoprens, Okap (Kap Ayisyen), Gonayiv, Jakmèl, Jeremi, Okay (Les Cayes), Sen Mak, Ench, Miragwàn, Fò Libète, Pòdepè, Leyogàn, Petyonvil, Site Solèy, Lagonav, Latòti, Il Avach, Sitadèl Laferyè, Vètyè, Bwa Kayiman, Lakayè, Mòn Lasèl, Pik Makaya, Latibonit, Lak Azuei, Pelerin...
- Department names: Lwès, Nò, Nòdès, Nòdwès, Sid, Sidès, Latibonit, Sant, Grandans, Nip.
- Keep sentences short and spoken-sounding. A 6-year-old must understand d=1 prompts.

## Content rules
- **Facts must be true and well established.** If you are not sure, leave the question out. Prefer famous, textbook facts over obscure ones. Do not invent dates or numbers. No "approximately" traps.
- **Kid-safe:** no violence detail, no vodou/lwa/zombi/folklore spirits, no religion, no politics after 1990, no living politicians, no alcohol/tobacco, no stereotypes. History of slavery and the revolution is fine at the level of a school textbook (who, what, when, why it mattered).
- Nothing already in the sample bank: Haiti independence year 1804; who built the Citadelle; date of Vertières; flag colors; capital of Haiti; number of departments; 1 January soup joumou; "Vil Powèt yo" (Jérémie); 7+5; 7×8; which planet we live on.
- Each prompt unique within and across categories. Don't ask the same fact from two directions more than once.
- For `matematik` write **word problems and reasoning** (money in goud, time, fractions, shapes, patterns, simple logic) — plain arithmetic drills are already generated by code. Numbers in options.
- Spread across the three difficulty levels roughly 40% d=1, 35% d=2, 25% d=3.

When done, report: count per file, the index spread of `a`, and a list of any question ids whose facts you were less than fully sure of (so they can be double-checked).

## Expansion round (target ≈2,000 questions)
- You write to your **own file** `content/bank/<category>.<part>.json` (e.g. `istwa.monn.json`). Never edit another file.
- Use **only the id range you were given** (e.g. `ist-201` … `ist-320`), sequential, zero-padded to 3 digits (4 when above 999).
- Before writing, read the existing files of your category (`content/bank/<category>*.json`) and do **not** repeat any fact already asked there. The checker warns on identical prompts; also avoid same-fact rewordings.
- Other writers are working on the same category in parallel on different sub-topics: stay inside your sub-topics.
- Write in batches of 25–40 (e.g. a small Node script that appends to your file), run the checker after each batch, and keep going until you reach your count.
- Kid level matters more than coverage: a d=1 question must be answerable by a curious 7-year-old in Haiti.
- If a fact needs a source you are not sure of, drop it; list anything borderline in your final report.
